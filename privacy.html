Rockya! Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Rockya! is a private, offline climbing journal. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Rockya! iOS app.

Information processed by the app

Rockya! processes the climbing photos you choose or take, along with journal information you enter, such as route grade, date, time, notes, route color, and marked hold positions. This information is used only to provide the app's route tracing, journal, calendar, collection, and progress features.

Local storage

Your photos and climbing records are stored locally on your device. Rockya! does not operate an account system or upload your journal to a Rockya! server. Your device or iCloud backup settings may separately back up app data under Apple's policies and your own device settings.

Camera and photo library access

If you grant permission, Rockya! uses the camera to take a climbing photo and the photo picker to let you choose an existing image. Selected images are processed on your device for route marking and are not transmitted by Rockya!.

Data collection and tracking

Rockya! does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information. The app does not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

Rockya! does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or cloud data services. The app's core features work without an internet connection.

Data retention and deletion

Climbing records remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Deleting a record removes the associated information from the app's local storage. Copies may remain in a device backup until that backup is replaced or deleted according to your Apple account and device settings.

Children's privacy

Rockya! does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because it does not transmit personal information to the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Rockya!'s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
